## Critical Learnings & Design Invariants
1. **Local-First & Sovereignty:** All proposed features (dynamic swarm geofencing, P2P mesh consensus, spatial drift prediction, immutable flight recorder, universal policy compiler) run locally on the tenant's infrastructure or edge nodes without exfiltrating location data or secrets to external clouds.
2. **UEM Complement Neutrality:** None of the visionary features attempt to turn LucidFence into an MDM/UEM. They audit, predict, and enforce geofencing/risk policies around existing UEM adapters (Intune, Jamf, Applivery, Fleet, Workspace ONE).
3. **$0 / Free Forever (Apache-2.0):** No commercial upgrade triggers, paywalls, or enterprise tiers are added.
4. **Zero Telemetry & Privacy by Design:** Location tracking remains ephemeral, minimal, and fully audited.
5. **Human-Gated Safety:** Destructive actions generated by spatial prediction or swarm breaches remain gated behind explicit human approval or double-key confirmation.
